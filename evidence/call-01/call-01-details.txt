Primary quality finding:
AI TTS audio was repeatedly recognized as incoming patient speech.

Examples:
- ACS recognized the bot's own greeting:
  "I'm here to help with appointments. Questions about the clinic or anything else you need? What can I do for you today?"

- ACS subsequently recognized another bot response:
  "I can help with that before I check the schedule. Would you like to create a demo patient profile?"

- ACS later recognized the bot's patient-profile response:
  "Your patient profile has been created. For demo purposes, your date of birth is July 4th, 2000."

- ACS later recognized the bot's appointment instructions:
  "Your appointment is set for Thursday, September 10th at 9:30 AM with Doctor Zbigniew Lukowski..."

- ACS finally recognized the bot's closing:
  "You're all set. If you have any other questions before your visit, just let me know. Take care."

This indicates insufficient separation between outbound bot audio and inbound
speech recognition.
