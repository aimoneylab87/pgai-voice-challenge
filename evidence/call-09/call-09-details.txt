CALL 09 — Medication Refill / Scheduling Edge Case

Date: September 20, 2026
Scenario: Medication refill request / patient interaction
Call result: Completed normally
Recording duration: Approximately 4 minutes 41 seconds
Recording format: MP3, 64 kbps, 16 kHz, mono
Recording storage: Azure Blob Storage

Observed behavior:
- The call completed and the recording was successfully finalized.
- The bot experienced significant speech-recognition contamination from the AI agent's own audio.
- Agent utterances were repeatedly interpreted as patient responses.
- The conversation drifted into appointment scheduling even though the intended scenario was a medication refill.
- Recognized agent speech included appointment questions, provider availability, appointment booking, and "Are you still there?" prompts.
- This produced an extended interaction loop and reduced confidence that the patient persona was actually driving the conversation.

Primary quality issue:
Audio/recognition isolation is insufficient. The bot can recognize the AI agent's TTS output as if it were patient speech, causing false turns and conversation drift.

Engineering significance:
This call provides additional evidence for the turn-taking and echo-contamination bug and demonstrates that the current architecture needs stronger speaker isolation and pipeline-level conversation control.
